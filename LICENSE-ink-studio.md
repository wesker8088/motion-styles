# ink-studio attribution (MIT)

The style `pixel-splat-diorama` (batch b53, 2026-11-21) is based on https://github.com/siliconjungle/shapeshift-labs-ink-studio by siliconjungle (main 1432518). Its 6-second sample was rendered by us, unchanged, with the repository's own CLI: `node cli/ink-studio.js clip cabinet --from 0 --to 6 --fps 30 --camera-pan 0,0,0 --camera-orbit 40 --w 640 --h 360 --px 2`, then nearest-neighbour scaled 2× to 1280×720 and re-encoded with a silent track. Style description rewritten by us.
