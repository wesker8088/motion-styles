# stick-scene attribution (MIT)

The style `stick-figure-machine-gag` (batch b54, 2026-11-22) is based on https://github.com/nilbuild/stick-scene by Kamran Ahmed (main ca84311). Its 6-second sample is the first 6 seconds of the repository's own `.github/yaml-sample.gif` (the README "Converter" example), scaled to 1280×640 and re-encoded as H.264 with a silent track.
