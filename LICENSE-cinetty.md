# cinetty attribution (MIT)

The style `terminal-cell-flow-board` (batch b43, 2026-11-11) uses a 6-second excerpt of the official hero example and ideas from this repository by Joseph Zhou:

- https://github.com/JosssphZhou/cinetty (`docs/hero.mp4`, 1280×720, 30 fps, 10 s; commit 673a06c): excerpt 2.0–8.0 s.

We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds, re-encoded as H.264, silent audio track added (the upstream file has no audio). The two CC0 street photos used by the upstream `photo-loop` template are not part of our excerpt.
