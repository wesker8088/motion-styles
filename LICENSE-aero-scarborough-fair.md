# aero-scarborough-fair attribution (MIT)

The style `herbal-ink-folk-ballad` (batch b57, 2026-11-25) is based on https://github.com/ChetasLua/aero-scarborough-fair by Chetas Lua (main d9f1a32). Its sample is self-rendered with the upstream engine: 18.0–24.0 s of the bundled `scarborough_fair_aero.html`, drawn in headless Chrome via `ArtScenes.renderFilmFrame` on its native 1280×720 logical canvas at 12 drawings per second (each held for two frames, 24 fps), with the matching 18.0–24.0 s of the embedded code-synthesised WAV (Scarborough Fair is a traditional, public-domain English folk ballad). Style description rewritten by us.
