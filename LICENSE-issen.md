# issen attribution (MIT; audio CC0 / CC BY)

The style `issen-one-flash` (batch b30, 2026-10-29) uses a 6-second excerpt (5.5–11.5 s) of the official film `media/ISSEN.mp4` from https://github.com/Leonxlnx/issen, and ideas from that repository's README. We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds and transcoded to 1280×720 at 30 fps (source is 1080p60).
