# cth9191/animate attribution (MIT)

The styles `manim-colorcode-proof` (upstream `math`) and `crosshatch-ink-explainer` (upstream `crosshatch`) (batch b36, 2026-11-04) are based on https://github.com/cth9191/animate by cth9191: ideas from `plugins/animate/skills/animate/styles/{math,crosshatch}/STYLE.md`, and samples rendered by us from the upstream's own `styles/<style>/demo` pieces with the upstream's own tools (`tools/build.mjs`, `tools/export.mjs`). Style descriptions were rewritten by us.

Changes made by us: the native 1080×1920 24 fps 9:16 demo renders were scaled to 720 px high, padded left/right with the stage colour (math #0E0E10, crosshatch #F4ECD9) to 1280×720, converted to 30 fps H.264/AAC. Audio is the upstream's code-synthesised score (OfflineAudioContext); no third-party recordings.

Notes: the `math` palette uses manim's colour constants (ManimCommunity/manim, MIT). Its small pi-shaped hero is a nod to the manim / 3Blue1Brown tradition, drawn in code by the upstream; replace it with your own mascot for commercial use. The `crosshatch` terracotta dot-with-asterisk hero comes from the upstream's Claude Code explainers; swap it for your own brand's character.
