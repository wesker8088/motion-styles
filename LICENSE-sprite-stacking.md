# sprite-stacking attribution (MIT)

The style `sprite-stack-pixel-turntable` (batch b53, 2026-11-21) is based on https://github.com/danielwh2/sprite-stacking by Daniel White (main 6fecdfd). Its 6-second sample is the first 6 seconds of the repository's official `media/showcase.gif`, nearest-neighbour scaled 2× to 1080×1080 and re-encoded with a silent track; picture unchanged. Style description rewritten by us.
