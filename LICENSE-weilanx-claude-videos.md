# weilanx-claude-videos attribution (MIT)

The style tagged `lamplight-history` (batch b17, 2026-10-22) is based on the 灯火史话风 (lamplight) style of https://github.com/Azure12355/weilanx-claude-videos by Azure12355 (commit fb2f5e4). Its sample is a 6-second excerpt (frames 15–194) of our own render of the repository's own episode `ep28-express-relay`, made with the repository's unmodified Remotion project; the scene code was not changed. The episode's music track (`we-never`) is not distributed with the repository, so the render used a silent music track — the only sound is the repository's code-synthesised SFX. `Songti SC` was mapped to Noto Serif CJK SC (OFL) via fontconfig. Changes made by us: excerpted to 6 seconds and transcoded to 1280×720. Style descriptions were rewritten by us.

Per the upstream README: code is MIT (text below); fonts in `public/fonts/` are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the art styles and narration grammars in `styles/` are adapted from alchaincyf/huashu-art-motion (MIT); the lamplight visual grammar was distilled by the author from two reference videos that are not in the repository, with all components rewritten.
