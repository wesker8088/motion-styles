# ClaudeAnimationBase attribution (MIT)

The style `brush-painted-cartoon` (batch b13, 2026-10-19) is based on https://github.com/JohnHeibel/ClaudeAnimationBase by John Heibel. Its 6-second sample was rendered, unchanged, from the kit's own demo scene `src/scenes/demo.js` ("The fallen star") with the kit's `render.mjs` (software GL; frames 3.5–9.5 s, 24 fps → 30 fps, scaled to 1280×720). Style description rewritten by us.
