# functional-emotions-video attribution (MIT)

The style `painted-stroke-night-mv` (batch b52, 2026-11-20) is based on https://github.com/ledbetterljoshua/functional-emotions-video by Joshua Ledbetter (main b34c20a). Its 6-second sample was rendered by us, unchanged, with the repository's own `render.mjs` (song time 2.5–8.5 s, software GL, 1920×1080 scaled to 1280×720), with the audio-muxing step removed. The song, its lyrics and the audio in the repository's `assets/` are not covered by the MIT licence and are not used or redistributed here. Style description rewritten by us.
