# BB-Video attribution (MIT)

The styles `bayeux-tapestry-scroll` (upstream `tapestry`), `manga-page-comic` (upstream `manga`) and `chat-log-narrative` (upstream `chat`) (batch b34, 2026-11-02) use 6-second excerpts of the official sample films from https://github.com/BubbleBrainBB/BB-Video by Dylan Deng (BubbleBrainBB), and ideas from the corresponding `skills/bb-video/styles/*/STYLE.md` files. Style descriptions were rewritten by us.

Batch b35 (2026-11-03): the styles `pencil-test-animation` (upstream `pencil`), `ink-world-wash` (upstream `drawnworld`), `lone-light-monologue` (upstream `lumen`) and `cinematic-trailer-cards` (upstream `trailer`) likewise use 6-second excerpts of the official sample films and ideas from the corresponding STYLE.md files; descriptions rewritten by us.

Licensing: the repository is MIT (text below). Official sample mp4s are published at https://github.com/BubbleBrainBB/BB-Video/releases/tag/samples and distributed with the project. Changes made by us: excerpted to 6 seconds and transcoded to 1280×720 H.264/AAC at 30 fps.

Sample windows: tapestry 《一本书是怎么来的》 ≈8.0–14.0 s; manga 《咖啡因的45分钟》 ≈5.0–11.0 s; chat 《一骑红尘妃子笑》 ≈2.0–8.0 s. Narration in these samples uses Microsoft edge-tts (replace the voice track for reuse if needed). Fonts used upstream are OFL (Noto Sans/Serif SC, LXGW WenKai, etc. as listed in each STYLE.md). The Bayeux Tapestry look referenced by `tapestry` is 11th-century public domain; figures in the sample are generic stitched characters drawn in code.

b35 sample windows: pencil *How the bicycle got its shape* ≈28.0–34.0 s; drawnworld 《为什么会有通货膨胀》 ≈70.0–76.0 s; lumen 《再刷五分钟 / Five More Minutes》 ≈11.0–17.0 s; trailer 《深空回声》 ≈50.0–56.0 s. Narration uses Microsoft edge-tts (replace the voice track for reuse if needed). The `lumen` protagonist is an original character of the upstream project (upstream forbids imitating other channels' signature figures); the `trailer` studio ident, film title and billing names are invented. `drawnworld` letters CJK with stroke data from hanzi-writer-data / Make Me a Hanzi (their own licences).
