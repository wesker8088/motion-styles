# Claude Video 2.0 (ItsssssJack/claude-video-2) attribution (MIT)

The styles `data-island-city`, `era-matchcut-timeline` and `point-cloud-morph` (batch b25, 2026-10-25) take ideas from the looks "A city that grows" (`styles/growing-city`), "30,000 years in 15 seconds" (`styles/time-travel`) and "Ten thousand points" (`styles/particle-field`) in https://github.com/ItsssssJack/claude-video-2 . Each one's sample is a 6-second excerpt of the official gallery video in the repo: `gallery/videos/growing-city.mp4` 6.0–12.0 s, `gallery/videos/time-travel.mp4` 1.5–7.5 s, `gallery/videos/particle-field.mp4` 5.5–11.5 s. We rewrote the style descriptions ourselves. Our changes: cut to 6 seconds and re-encoded as H.264 at 30 fps with a silent AAC track added (the originals have no sound). The picture was not changed. The fonts used upstream (Inter, Libre Baskerville, Tenor Sans, Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display, Jost, JetBrains Mono) keep their own OFL licences.
