# bide attribution (MIT)

The style `red-thread-sung-ballad` (batch b57, 2026-11-25) is based on https://github.com/ChetasLua/bide by ChetasLua (main d581a30). Its sample is self-rendered with the upstream engine: frames 50.0–56.0 s of the bundled `dist/bide.html`, captured in headless Chrome via `BIDE.init` + `BIDE.renderAt(n/12)` (the method of `tools/record.mjs`), scaled to 1280×720, with the matching 50.0–56.0 s of the embedded code-synthesised `song.flac` (Danny Boy: words Frederic Weatherly 1913 and the traditional Londonderry Air, both public domain). Style description rewritten by us.
