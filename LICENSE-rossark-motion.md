# rossark-motion-skill (rossark0/rossark-motion-skill) attribution (MIT)

The style `app-mascot-acting` (batch b28, 2026-10-27) takes ideas from https://github.com/rossark0/rossark-motion-skill ("Motion For Anything You Draw"). Its sample is a 6-second excerpt (0.0–6.0 s) of the official demo `demo/garden.mp4` (1080×1080, 60 fps, 10 s loop, drawn and animated in SwiftUI code by the author). We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: cut to 6 seconds, scaled to 720 px high and padded with white to 1280×720, re-encoded as H.264 at 30 fps with a silent AAC track added (the original has no sound). The picture content was not changed. Heat reference: X post by @rossark0 https://x.com/rossark0/status/2114074426949968237 .
