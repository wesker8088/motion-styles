# iching-64-motion attribution (MIT; fonts excluded)

The style `iching-hexagram-structure` (batch b30, 2026-10-29) uses a 6-second excerpt (52.0–58.0 s) of the official film `iching-64-motion.mp4` from https://github.com/jianxinss/iching-64-motion Releases v1.0, and ideas from that repository's README. We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds and transcoded to 1280×720 at 30 fps.
