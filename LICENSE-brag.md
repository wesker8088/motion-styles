# brag attribution (MIT)

The style `deadpan-parody-launch` (batch b55, 2026-11-23) is based on https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag by Shunit Haviv Hakimi (main 0637943). Its sample is the repository's own example film `docs/examples/taxi-for-taxis/brag.mp4` (22 s), scaled to 1280×720 and re-encoded as H.264 with the original music and sound effects removed (the bundled music is ende.app "Happy Beats / Business Moves", CC BY 4.0, and is not used here) and a silent track added. Style description rewritten by us.
