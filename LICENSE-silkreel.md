# silkreel attribution (Apache-2.0)

The style `pcb-silk-reel` (batch b30, 2026-10-29) uses a 6-second excerpt (4.0–10.0 s of the 12 s GIF) of the official sample `.github/media/glasgow-trace.gif` from https://github.com/hridaya423/silkreel (Glasgow rev C3 board), and ideas from that repository's README. We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: loop-trimmed/excerpted to 6 seconds, upscaled from 480×270 to 1280×720, and transcoded to h264+aac at 30 fps (added a silent aac track; original GIF has no audio in the excerpt pipeline).

Sample boards credited by upstream include Glasgow Embedded Glasgow rev C3 (0BSD) among others; see upstream README Credits. Sound for full trace films is marked CC0 by upstream; this library's 6 s sample was cut from the GIF and does not carry that score.
