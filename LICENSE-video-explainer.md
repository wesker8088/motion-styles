# video-explainer attribution (MIT)

The style `single-take-3d-explainer` (batch b58, 2026-11-26) is based on https://github.com/petergyang/video-explainer by Peter Yang (main be2f729). Its sample is the 3D segment (12.0–20.07 s) of the upstream README's official "/video-explainer how do black holes form, in each style" demo video, scaled from 1920×1080 to 1280×720, re-encoded to 24 fps and muted (the original soundtrack is not used). Bundled Inter font is under the SIL Open Font License. Style description rewritten by us.
