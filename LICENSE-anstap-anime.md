# anstapnever anime shorts attribution (MIT)

The style `cel-paint-anime-short` (batch b42, 2026-11-10) uses a 6-second excerpt of the official film and ideas from this repository by anstapnever (X @anstapnever):

- https://github.com/anstapnever/anchor (film `film/anchor.mp4`, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 92 s; commit d80d81a): excerpt 50.0–56.0 s. The score is synthesized by the repository's own code (`audio/score.mjs`) and is covered by the same licence.
- Cited as related work only (no media used): https://github.com/anstapnever/colossus , https://github.com/anstapnever/one-sweep , https://github.com/anstapnever/blackout , https://github.com/anstapnever/star-catcher , https://github.com/anstapnever/paper-plane (MIT).

We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds, transcoded to 1280×720 at 30 fps, 0.15 s / 0.2 s audio fade in / out.
