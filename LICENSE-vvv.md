The style `dv-viewfinder-memoir` (batch b23, 2026-10-24) uses a 6-second excerpt (17.0–23.0 s) of the official template preview `docs/assets/dv-story.mp4` and ideas from the "DV Story" template in https://github.com/actionow-ai/vvv . We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds and upscaled/transcoded to 1280×720 at 30 fps.
