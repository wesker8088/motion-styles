# inkworks attribution (MIT)

The style `instrumented-drafting-sheet-explainer` (batch b59, 2026-11-27) is based on https://github.com/Moh4696/inkworks-skill by exploraX (main 8769b7d). Its sample is the author's official `examples/engine-walkthrough.webp` (animated WebP, 960×540, 133 frames, ~11.95 s) converted frame-by-frame with the original per-frame durations to H.264 at 1280×720 with a silent audio track (the original has no sound). three.js is vendored by upstream under its own MIT licence. Style description rewritten by us.
