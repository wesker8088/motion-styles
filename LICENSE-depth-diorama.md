# depth-diorama attribution (MIT)

The style `poster-unfold-diorama` (batch b58, 2026-11-26) is based on https://github.com/hardikverma22/depth-diorama by Hardik Verma (main b999162). Its sample is the upstream README's official first example video ("A mountain ski village at night in falling snow", 12.1 s, silent), re-encoded to 24 fps / higher compression with a silent audio track added; no cropping. Style description rewritten by us.
