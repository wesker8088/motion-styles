# cockpit-ui attribution (MIT)

The style `mecha-cockpit-cel-ui` (batch b58, 2026-11-26) is based on https://github.com/finktheartist/cockpit-ui by Justin Finkelman (main d790a1a). Its sample is self-recorded from the unmodified upstream `launch` control (launch/index.html?embed=1) in headless Chromium (Playwright record_video, 1280×720 viewport, page zoom 1.5) while scripting one guard-lift + hold-to-launch interaction, trimmed to 9 s with a silent audio track. The control and all its paintings are upstream's, unmodified. Style description rewritten by us.
