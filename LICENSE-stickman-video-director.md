# stickman-video-director attribution (MIT)

The style `pixel-theater-choice-fable` (batch b53, 2026-11-21) is based on the B7 "Pixel Theater" module of https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director (main 7fa6a9a). Its 6-second sample is cut from the repository's official README film `assets/readme/b7-pixel-theater.mp4` (4–10 s, nearest-neighbour scaled to 1280×720, re-encoded); the narration and music were removed and replaced with a silent track. The music in the original film (Eric Skiff "Underclocked (underunderclocked mix)", Kevin MacLeod "Gymnopedie No. 1", both CC BY 4.0) is not used or redistributed here. Style description rewritten by us.
