# anidoodle attribution (Apache-2.0)

The styles `charcoal-erasure-palimpsest` (upstream style `charcoalErasure`), `blueprint-comes-alive` (upstream worked example *Mechanical Lepidoptera*) and `draw-along-lesson` (upstream teach-drawing workflow, owl lesson) (batch b37, 2026-11-05) are based on https://github.com/alexgreensh/anidoodle by Alex Greenshpun. Ideas come from `skills/anidoodle/references/styles/charcoalErasure.md`, `skills/anidoodle/references/styles/blueprint.md`, `skills/anidoodle/example/README.md`, `skills/anidoodle/example/butterfly-film-spec.md`, `skills/anidoodle/references/workflows/teach-drawing.md` and the repository README. We rewrote all style descriptions ourselves.

Samples and our changes:
- `charcoal-erasure-palimpsest`: rendered by us from the upstream's own demo with the upstream's own engine (`skills/anidoodle/engine`, `node tools/render.mjs charcoalErasure`; native 1080×1080 30 fps, 18 s, no audio). Excerpt 9.5–15.5 s, scaled to 720 px high, padded left/right with #CFCBC2 to 1280×720, H.264 with a silent AAC track added.
- `blueprint-comes-alive`: excerpt 19.0–25.0 s of the official film `assets/mechanical-lepidoptera.mp4` (1080×1080 30 fps, 47.0 s; picture and score are generated by code, per the upstream README). Scaled to 720 px high, padded left/right with #0F2747 to 1280×720, H.264/AAC; picture content unchanged.
- `draw-along-lesson`: excerpt 51.0–57.0 s of the official lesson film `assets/readme/lesson.mp4` (1080×1656 30 fps, 94.5 s, no audio). Scaled to 720 px high, padded left/right with #EFEBE2 to 1280×720, H.264 with a silent AAC track added; picture content unchanged.
