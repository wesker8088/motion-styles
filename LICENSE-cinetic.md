# cinetic attribution (MIT)

The styles `white-keygrid-sequencer-world` and `red-diff-brick-wall-teaser` (batch b59, 2026-11-27) are based on https://github.com/Leonxlnx/cinetic by Leonxlnx (main f4bbfc8; root LICENSE and skills/cinetic/LICENSE.txt are both the standard MIT text). Their samples are the author's own official example films from the repository's Releases v1.1.0 (`cinetic-1.1-update.mp4` 0–6.4 s and `kiln-launch-teaser.mp4`, full), re-encoded to 1280×720 30 fps with the original audio (licensed music and sound effects) removed and replaced by a silent track. The films show only the fictional product kiln and cinetic's own name and logo. Style descriptions rewritten by us.
