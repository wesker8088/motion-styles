The style `sumi-dark-red-brush` (batch b43, 2026-11-11) uses a 6-second video-only excerpt of an official example film and ideas from this repository by Muhammad Tahir Ashraf (Beyond Tahir):

- https://github.com/beyondtahir/beyondsumi (Release v2.0.0 asset `beyondsumi-dark.mp4`, 1920×1080, 24 fps, 29.3 s, "The Third Knock"; main commit 6f8ec72): excerpt 21.0–27.0 s.

We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds, scaled to 1280×720 at 30 fps, **audio removed** (upstream THIRD-PARTY.md says the finished films may contain separately licensed generated narration or integrated effects and that these are not relicensed), silent audio track added.
