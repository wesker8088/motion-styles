# hand-drawn-canvas-animation attribution (MIT)

The styles `museum-photo-doodle` and `found-motion-rotoscope` (batch b52, 2026-11-20) are based on the `skills/hand-drawn-canvas-animation` skill in https://github.com/alesha-pro/tools by Alexey Fateev (main 90e9662). Their 6-second samples were rendered by us, unchanged, with the skill's own `scripts/render.mjs`: `examples/held-once.html` (4.0–10.0 s) and `examples/gallop.html` (13.0–19.0 s), 1080×1080 scaled to 720×720, silent. Third-party material in those samples: photographs from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access (CC0) — Pocket watch, ca. 1900 (https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/206812) and Antonio Stradivari, "Gould" violin, 1693 (https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/503045); motion traced from Eadweard Muybridge, Descriptive Zoopraxography (1893), public domain. The doodle look was rebuilt by the skill author from a film by Kevin Ngo (https://x.com/kevin_t_ngo/status/2100601972902842517); that film is not used or redistributed here. Style descriptions rewritten by us.
