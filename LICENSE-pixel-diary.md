# pixel-diary (garycaogan/pixel-diary) attribution (MIT)

The style `pixel-diary-dialog` (batch b28, 2026-10-27) takes ideas from https://github.com/garycaogan/pixel-diary by Gary (曹淦). The repo ships stills only, so the sample was rendered by us with the repo's own engine, unchanged: we kept 4 of the 11 scenes of the official episode `episodes/demo/day0.json` (title, handover, status, ledger — no text edited), built it with `python -m pixel_diary build` and rendered with HyperFrames 0.8.181 at 1920×1080 30 fps, then cut 13.9–19.9 s (the 今日账本 ledger with the 只能往上了 stamp), scaled to 1280×720 and loudness-normalised. The character art (`cast/claude`) and all sounds and 8-bit music are from the repo (the README says the sounds and music are synthesised by the engine). Font: Noto Sans SC (SIL OFL 1.1), subset by the engine. Upstream notes the series is an AI experiment and not an official Anthropic account.
