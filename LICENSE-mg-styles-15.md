# mg-styles-15 attribution (MIT)

The samples/styles tagged `cyber-hud-fui`, `variety-hanazi` and `soft-3d-cloner` (batch b15, 2026-10-21) use 6-second excerpts of the official films and ideas from https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/mg-styles-15 by Vincentwei1021 (films `22-hud`, `18-hanazi`, `04-3d-render`). Style descriptions were rewritten by us. Changes made by us: excerpted to 6 seconds and transcoded (1280×720, or 720×1280 for the vertical film).

Licensing as stated in the upstream README: the code, prompts, docs and films made for that project are released under the MIT License (text below). Third-party files keep their own licences: the instrument samples (VCSL), the HDRI (Poly Haven studio_small_09), the paper texture, the photos (the cat photos in `18-hanazi`) and the HUD film's map data (Natural Earth) are CC0 or public domain; the glyph outlines in `demos/04-3d-render/assets/mesh/` come from fonts under the SIL Open Font License and stay under it.
