# Paper Fox attribution (MIT)

The style `paper-multiplane-journey` (batch b24, 2026-10-24) uses a 6-second excerpt (15.0–21.0 s) of the official release film `paper-fox.mp4` (release v1.0, 1920×1080 60 fps, 60 s) and ideas from https://github.com/Leonxlnx/paper-fox by Leon Lin (@LexnLin). We rewrote the style description ourselves. Our changes: excerpted to 6 seconds and transcoded to 1280×720 at 30 fps.

Audio in the excerpt: the music is "Fairy Tale" by Music_For_Videos via Pixabay (https://pixabay.com/music/fantasy-dreamy-childrens-fairy-tale-164242/), under the Pixabay Content License; the sound effects are from Mixkit (https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/), under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License.
